Three buckets, in order of size — note which are *structural* vs *tunable*:

A pre-market-only scan cannot see these by construction. Recovering them needs an **intraday re-scan**, not a threshold change — this, not the sub-$20 rule, is the real ceiling on catch rate.
2. **Rank / board-size cut (~37%) — tunable.** Names that *were* visible but ranked below the board's cut (prelim top-30 → final top-5). This bucket includes **validated `rider`-class movers that missed by a handful of ranks** (e.g. OUST (+9.5%), AAOI (+9.3%), FCEL (+9.3%)). Widening the board or improving the score recovers these directly.
3. **Deliberate class + hard gates (~24%).** The `caution`/`avoid` badge cut (sub-$20, failed validation — *correctly* skipped) **plus two hard gates**: the **$150 price ceiling**, which cut validated riders (CBRS (+12.2%), NBIS (+10.3%), CBRS (+7.1%)), and the pre-market dollar-volume floor. The price cap in particular is discarding validated-class opportunity.

**Correction to the intuitive story:** the low catch rate is *not* mainly the sub-$20 exclusion — `BADGE_CUT` is only ~11% of movers. It is dominated by **invisible-at-0830** (can't be tuned away without an intraday pass) and **rank / price-cap cuts of validated names** (which can). Spend enhancement effort on the tunable buckets.

**The number worth optimizing:** not headline board-catch, but **"of the validated-class names with a real intraday ride, how many did we catch?"** — which the rank-widening and price-cap fixes move directly.
